# 01 · Positioning & Messaging

## The wedge: a niche nobody else owns

GovCon software is crowded (~30 competitors), but almost all of them are built
for **one company bidding for itself** or for **enterprise buyers with big
budgets**. Bytescon is built for the **consulting firm managing many small-business
clients at once** — and gives that firm a white-label portal to put its own brand
in front of those clients.

> **Positioning statement**
> For GovCon consulting and capture firms that juggle many small-business clients,
> Bytescon is the all-in-one contracting workspace that finds opportunities, scores
> win odds, enforces compliance, drafts proposals, and runs post-award finance —
> under your own brand — for a fraction of what enterprise tools cost. Unlike
> single-company tools (CLEATUS, GovDash) or six-figure suites (Deltek, Unanet),
> Bytescon is multi-client by design, post-award-aware, and transparently
> human-in-the-loop.

### Why this wins
- **Multi-client + white-label + client portal** — no competitor checked combines
  all three. This is the defensible wedge.
- **Post-award finance built in** (DCAA timekeeping, invoicing, cash flow) — rare
  among discovery/proposal competitors; it extends customer lifetime value because
  firms stay after the win, not just during the hunt.
- **Price** — $99/month Core vs. $30K–$100K+/yr quote-based rivals. 3–5× cheaper,
  and published (no "contact sales" wall).
- **Opt-in AI agents** — firms pick which of the 9 agents run and at what autonomy,
  within an AI budget. Positions against "black-box autonomous" rivals.

## The message house

**Roof (the promise):** *Know your odds before you bid — and run every client's
whole contract lifecycle in one place.*

**Three pillars (what we repeat everywhere):**

1. **See more, chase smarter.**
   Auto-matched opportunities from SAM.gov, Grants.gov, agency forecasts,
   USAspending, FPDS, SBA SUBNet, and state/local portals — scored by a 9-factor
   win-probability engine that returns a clear **Bid Prime / Bid Sub / No Bid** call.
   *Proof:* re-compete detection, forecast-to-solicitation linking, calibration
   against your own won/lost record.

2. **One workspace per client, one brand: yours.**
   Separate workspaces for every client company, a branded client portal, custom
   domain, branded PDFs and emails. Your clients see *your* firm, not ours.
   *Proof:* unlimited client companies on All Access; white-label domains.

3. **From search to signed to paid.**
   Compliance matrix (Section L/M, FAR/DFARS), proposal + pricing + submission,
   teaming and e-signatures, then contracts, deliverables, DCAA-style timekeeping,
   invoicing, and cash-flow projection.
   *Proof:* the full Discover → Qualify → Comply → Propose → Perform loop lives in
   one tool.

**Foundation (trust, under everything):**
*AI suggests, people decide.* No agent submits bids, signs papers, approves
invoices, or emails partners on its own. *We do not train AI models on your data.*
Security: SOC 2 and CMMC L2 in progress.

## Objection handling

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| *"You don't have FedRAMP / SOC 2."* | "SOC 2 and CMMC L2 are in progress; we don't train AI on your data, and every AI action is human-approved. For most consulting firms managing CUI-light pipelines, that's the right posture today — and we'll tell you honestly where we are." (Don't bluff; for FedRAMP-required buyers, log it as roadmap.) |
| *"We already use Deltek / Unanet."* | "Those are six-figure back-office suites. Bytescon adds the front of the funnel — discovery, scoring, proposals — and a client portal, at 1/30th the price. Many firms run us alongside until they consolidate." |
| *"$99 seems too cheap to be real."* | "It's published because we're not an enterprise-sales company — we want firms to try it in 14 days with no card. Price goes up only if you add modules you actually use." |
| *"Will AI make mistakes on my bids?"* | "AI never submits or signs. It drafts and flags; a human approves everything. You also set an AI budget and choose which agents run." |
| *"Switching tools is painful."* | "CSV import for clients, a one-click demo workspace, and a 14-day trial. Start with one client, not your whole book." |
| *"No Word add-in / no mobile app."* | Acknowledge honestly: "Not yet — it's on the roadmap. Export to PDF works today; the proposal workspace is browser-based and installable as a PWA." |
