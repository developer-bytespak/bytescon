# 06 · Metrics, Budget & Tool Stack

## The funnel & the math

Track the whole path so you know which channel earns its keep.

```
Reach  →  Site visit  →  Lead (email/trial)  →  Activated  →  Paying  →  Retained/Referred
(LinkedIn,   (landing     (lead magnet,         (loaded a     (Core/      (stays >3mo,
 content,     page)        free trial)           workspace,    All Access)  refers)
 outreach)                                       scored a bid)
```

### North-star metric
**Number of consulting firms running ≥1 real client workspace and paying.** Not
signups — *activated, paying firms.* That's what proves the wedge.

### KPIs by stage

| Stage | Metric | Early target (first 90 days) |
|-------|--------|------------------------------|
| Reach | LinkedIn followers; post impressions | 1,000 relevant followers |
| Traffic | Unique site visitors/mo | 2,000 by month 3 |
| Lead | Email leads + trial starts | 100 leads; 40 trials |
| Activation | Trials that score a bid / load a client | 50% of trials |
| Conversion | Trial → paid | 15–25% of trials |
| Revenue | Paying firms; MRR | 10–20 paying firms |
| Retention | Logo retention at 90 days | >80% |
| Referral | Referred trials | ≥10% of new trials |

### Activation is the leverage point
A trial that **loads a client, runs discovery, and sees a win score** converts far
better than one that just logs in. The product has a **"Load demo"** button (8 sample
opportunities) and an onboarding wizard — make hitting the first win-score the
explicit activation goal of onboarding emails and in-app nudges.

## Pricing as a growth lever

The published, aggressive pricing is a marketing weapon — use these deliberately:

- **$99/month Core + 14-day trial, no credit card** — the frictionless top of funnel.
  Lead ads/content to "try free, no card."
- **Add-on modules** ($29–$79/mo) — land on Core, **expand** as firms adopt compliance,
  proposal, teaming, state/local. Market expansion to existing customers (in-app +
  email) — cheaper than acquiring new logos.
- **All Access $199/mo** — position as the no-brainer for firms with many clients
  (unlimited client companies, every module, 50 proposal tokens).
- **Founders Lifetime ($2,500, 20 slots)** — a genuine launch urgency play. Promote it
  hard during beta/launch to early believers; "only 20 ever" is real scarcity and
  early cash. Track slots remaining publicly.
- **Veteran-owned 10% discount** — a targeting hook for veteran business networks.
- **Proposal-token packs** — natural upsell for active proposal writers.

**Expansion-revenue motion:** most LTV growth here comes from Core → add-ons → All
Access, not from net-new logos. Build an in-product "you're hitting a limit / this
module would help" nudge and an email track for it.

## Lean budget (solo founder, pre-revenue)

Spend as little as possible until a landing page converts organically. Rough monthly
tiers:

| Tier | Monthly | What it buys |
|------|---------|--------------|
| **Bootstrap (now)** | ~$0–50 | Free LinkedIn, organic content, manual outreach, free tiers of tools below |
| **Growing (post-launch)** | ~$200–500 | Email platform, scheduling tool, LinkedIn Sales Navigator, 1 newsletter sponsorship |
| **Scaling (proven channel)** | ~$500–2,000 | Small LinkedIn/Google ad tests, design help, webinar tools, directory ads |

**Spend rule:** don't scale a channel until it shows a repeatable cost-per-trial you'd
pay again. Kill losers in 2–4 weeks.

> Any actual spend, paid tool, or ad account needs the owner's (BytesPak's) explicit
> go-ahead — this doc proposes, it doesn't authorize.

## Monthly review (keep yourself honest)

Once a month, answer:
1. Which channel produced the most **activated trials**? (Not vanity reach.)
2. What's the trial→paid rate, and where do trials drop off?
3. Which content/posts drove real pipeline? Do more of that; stop the rest.
4. Is expansion revenue (add-ons) growing per customer?
5. What did customers say in NPS / churn interviews — and what one thing will you fix?

Let the data pick the 1–2 channels to concentrate on. A solo founder wins by focus,
not by running every channel at once.
