## The one-sentence pitch

Bytescon helps GovCon consulting firms run every client's pipeline — discover
opportunities, score win probability, check compliance, draft proposals, and
handle post-award finance — in one white-labeled workspace, at **$99/month**
instead of the **$30K–$100K+/year** enterprise tools charge.

**Tagline in use:** *"Know your odds before you bid."*
**Core principle (a selling point):** *AI suggests, people decide.*

## The three things that will actually move the needle

Everything in this folder supports three bets. If you only do three things, do these:

1. **Own the "firms with many clients" niche.** No competitor checked combines
   multi-client workspaces + white-label client portal + post-award finance.
   Every page, post, and demo should say *"built for firms with many clients,
   not one."* Don't market to everyone in GovCon — market to the consultant who
   juggles 5–30 small-business clients and drowns in admin.

2. **Lead with price + payback, close with trust.** $99/month is a shock next to
   $30K+ quote-based competitors. Use it to start conversations. Close them with
   the *AI-suggests-people-decide* story and the honest security roadmap (SOC 2 /
   CMMC L2 in progress) — GovCon buyers are risk-averse.

3. **Turn 5 beta firms into 5 case studies.** In this market, a named firm saying
   "we scored 3 bids in an afternoon and won one we'd have skipped" outperforms
   any ad. Instrument the beta to produce proof, testimonials, and referrals.

## What we can honestly claim today (and what we can't yet)

Marketing must match reality or GovCon buyers will churn and talk.

**Claim now (live):** multi-client workspaces, white-label portals, win-probability
scoring with bid/no-bid calls, SAM.gov + Grants.gov + state/local discovery,
compliance matrix, proposal + pricing + submission, DCAA-style timekeeping and
invoicing, 9 opt-in AI agents, QuickBooks sync, MCP + public API.

**Say "in progress," never "done":** SOC 2 and CMMC L2 certification, production
file storage + field encryption hardening (close these before the public launch
lands enterprise prospects).

**Don't claim at all yet (known gaps vs. competitors — see doc 14):** FedRAMP
authorization, Microsoft Word add-in, a 1–2M government-buyer contact database,
AI pink/red-team proposal review, native iOS/Android apps, Salesforce/HubSpot
sync. Treat these as roadmap, not features — and as reasons a given prospect
might wait.

## Guardrails

- **Honesty over hype.** The product's own trust page says *"We do not train AI
  models on your data."* and *"SOC 2 and CMMC L2 in progress."* Keep marketing to
  that standard — overclaiming in GovCon ends deals and reputations.
- **Pre-launch, don't send mass outreach from production.** Staging is internal
  (`@bytesplatform.com` only). Build lists and warm up now; send at launch.
- **This is a plan, not spend authority.** Anything involving money, ads, paid
  tools, or outreach that represents the company should get Kamran's go-ahead
  first.
