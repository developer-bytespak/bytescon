# 04 · Content & SEO Engine

Content is how a solo founder earns trust at scale in a risk-averse market. The goal:
be the firm that *teaches* GovCon consultants to win — so Bytescon is the obvious tool
when they're ready to buy.

---

## Strategy: teach the loop, rank for the pain

Mirror the product loop — **Discover → Qualify → Comply → Propose → Perform** — in
content pillars. Every piece answers a real question an ICP firm Googles or asks,
then naturally shows how Bytescon does it.

### Pillar 1 — Discover
- "How to find federal contracts that actually fit your client's NAICS"
- "SAM.gov is free — so why do firms still miss opportunities?"
- "How to spot a re-compete before the solicitation drops"
- Keywords: *SAM.gov tracking, federal opportunity alerts, re-compete tracking,
  Grants.gov monitoring, state and local bid tracking.*

### Pillar 2 — Qualify (your signature topic)
- "How to calculate win probability for a federal bid (the 9 factors that matter)"
- "Bid/No-Bid: the decision that saves firms thousands in capture costs"
- "Why your win rate is lying to you (and how calibration fixes it)"
- Keywords: *win probability government contracts, bid no bid decision,
  capture management for small business, pWin calculation.*
- This pillar maps to the tagline. Own it. It's the most defensible content wedge.

### Pillar 3 — Comply
- "Section L vs. Section M, explained with examples"
- "Build a compliance matrix in 30 minutes"
- "FAR/DFARS clauses small firms get wrong most often"
- "Registration & certification expirations that quietly kill eligibility"
- Keywords: *compliance matrix template, Section L M, FAR clause checklist,
  SAM registration renewal.*

### Pillar 4 — Propose
- "Past-performance libraries: stop rewriting the same narrative"
- "Pricing a federal bid: fringe, overhead, G&A without a PhD"
- "How much should a small firm spend writing a proposal?"
- Keywords: *proposal management small business, BOE basis of estimate,
  government pricing proposal.*

### Pillar 5 — Perform
- "DCAA timekeeping for firms that just won their first contract"
- "Invoicing the government without getting rejected"
- "Contract health: options, mods, deliverables you can't miss"
- Keywords: *DCAA compliant timekeeping, government invoicing, contract management
  small business.*

### Cross-cutting: "vs." and comparison content
GovCon buyers comparison-shop hard. Write honest comparisons:
- "Bytescon vs. GovDash vs. CLEATUS: which GovCon tool for a multi-client firm?"
- "Alternatives to Deltek GovWin for small consulting firms"
- "Best GovCon software for firms managing multiple clients"
These rank for high-intent, bottom-funnel searches. Be fair — credibility sells.

## Formats & cadence (realistic for a solo founder)

| Format | Cadence | Purpose |
|--------|---------|---------|
| LinkedIn posts | 3–5 / week | Reach, authority (see [03](03-marketing-channels.md)) |
| Blog / SEO article | 1–2 / week | Search traffic, trust |
| Lead magnet (template/tool) | 1 / month | Email capture |
| Webinar | 1 / month | Demo + list growth |
| Case study | as wins land | Bottom-funnel proof |
| Short demo videos (Loom) | ongoing | Embed on site, DMs, replies |

**Repurpose everything:** one webinar → one blog post → five LinkedIn posts → one
lead magnet → clips. Write once, publish five times.

**Use the product + Claude to produce content faster** — but keep the founder's
voice and verify every GovCon fact (FAR cites, thresholds, program rules). Wrong
facts destroy credibility here faster than anywhere.

## SEO foundations (set up once)

1. **Blog on the primary domain** (`bytesgov.com/blog` or `/learn`) so links build
   domain authority where the app lives.
2. **Keyword map:** one target keyword per article, grouped by the five pillars
   above. Start with low-competition long-tails ("compliance matrix template for
   small business") before broad heads ("government contracting software").
3. **On-page basics:** clear title tags, meta descriptions, H1/H2 structure, internal
   links between pillar articles, fast load, mobile-friendly (the app is already a
   PWA — match that polish on the site).
4. **Lead magnets gated by email** on every article; every article ends with a soft
   CTA to the free trial.
5. **Schema + sitemap**; submit to Google Search Console; track rankings monthly.
6. **Backlinks:** guest posts on GovCon blogs, podcast show-notes links, APEX/SBA
   resource pages, directory listings.

4. **GovCon Proposal Checklist** (pre-submission validation on paper).
5. **DCAA Timekeeping Starter Guide** for newly-awarded small firms.

Each magnet → email nurture sequence → trial invite.

## Email nurture (once you have a list)

Short 5-email sequence after a lead magnet / trial signup:
1. Deliver the magnet + one quick win.
2. The pain story (chasing no-win bids) + the win-odds idea.
3. A customer/case-study proof point.
4. "Here's what the 14-day trial sets up for you" + demo video.
5. Soft offer (book a 15-min win-odds review).

Keep it educational, not salesy. Respect the honesty guardrail — no overclaiming on
security or roadmap features.
