# 03 · Marketing Channels — Ranked, with Action Steps

Channels ranked by expected ROI **for a solo founder, pre-revenue, in GovCon**.
Do the top tier first. Each has concrete, do-this-now steps.

---

## Tier 1 — Do these first (highest ROI, low/no cost)

### 1. Founder-led LinkedIn (the single best GovCon channel)
GovCon decision-makers live on LinkedIn. A founder posting real insight outperforms
any ad budget at this stage.

**Action steps**
1. Optimize Kamran's profile: headline = "Helping GovCon consulting firms win more
   with less admin · Founder, Bytescon." Banner = tagline. Featured = demo video +
   trial link.
2. Post 3–5×/week. Content mix: (a) GovCon tips (how to read Section M, how to spot
   a re-compete), (b) build-in-public (what you're shipping), (c) customer stories,
   (d) strong opinions ("most firms chase bids they can't win — here's the math").
3. Comment daily on 10 posts from GovCon leaders and target prospects. Comments >
   posts for cold reach early.
4. DM, don't pitch: when someone engages, start a conversation, offer a free
   "win-odds review" of one of their live pursuits (great product demo hook).
5. Create a **Bytescon company page**; re-share founder posts; keep it alive.

**Target:** 1,000 relevant followers and 10 inbound demo requests in 90 days.

### 2. Direct founder outreach to the ICP (warm, personal, not spammy)
With a short buying chain, personal outreach converts.

**Action steps**
1. Build a 200-firm named list (methods in [02](02-ideal-customer-profile.md)).
2. Warm first: follow, engage with their content for a week before reaching out.
3. Personalized message (LinkedIn or email): reference their firm/clients, offer a
   free win-probability review or a 14-day trial with a pre-loaded demo workspace.
4. Track in a simple CRM (the product has a CRM — or a spreadsheet to start).
5. **Pre-launch rule:** do not send bulk automated email from production; warm the
   list now, send real outreach at/after launch (staging is internal-only).

**Target:** 50 personalized touches/week → 5–8 trials/week post-launch.

### 3. APEX Accelerators & SBA resource-partner partnerships
APEX Accelerators (ex-PTACs), SBDCs, and SCORE counsel exactly the small businesses
your ICP serves. They're trusted, free to small firms, and always need tools to
recommend.

**Action steps**
1. List APEX Accelerators in target states; email the center directors.
2. Offer a free webinar/workshop ("How small firms can score their bids before
   spending capture hours") and a trial/discount for their clients.
3. Give counselors free accounts so they recommend Bytescon firsthand.
4. Ask for a listing in their resource directories.

**Target:** 3 partnerships + 2 co-hosted webinars in 90 days.

### 4. Beta → case studies → referrals (the compounding engine)
Your 3–5 beta firms are the most valuable marketing asset you'll have.

**Action steps**
1. Over-serve them. Weekly check-ins; fix friction fast.
2. Instrument wins: capture "scored X bids, skipped Y no-wins, won Z" stories.
3. Turn each into a 1-page case study + a 60-sec video testimonial + a LinkedIn post.
4. Add a referral incentive (e.g., a free month or bonus proposal tokens per
   referred firm that trials).

**Target:** 3 case studies + a referral loop live before public launch.

## Tier 2 — Build in parallel (compounding, moderate effort)

### 5. Content & SEO
Full plan in [04-content-and-seo.md](04-content-and-seo.md). GovCon buyers Google
their problems ("how to calculate win probability," "SAM.gov alternatives,"
"compliance matrix template"). Own those searches.

### 6. Free tools & lead magnets
A free, genuinely useful tool is the best top-of-funnel in GovCon.

**Ideas (ship one):**
- **Free win-probability "quick score"** — paste a SAM.gov notice + your NAICS/certs,
  get an odds estimate + a nudge to sign up for the full engine.
- **NAICS / set-aside eligibility checker.**
- **Compliance-matrix / Section L-M starter template** (gated by email).
- **Bid/No-Bid scorecard template.**

Each captures an email and demonstrates the core product.

### 7. Webinars & live demos
Monthly webinar on a real pain ("Reading Section L & M without missing a
disqualifier," "Post-award DCAA basics for small firms"). Co-host with APEX/an
influencer when possible. Record → chop into clips → gate the replay.

### 8. Podcasts & newsletter sponsorships / guesting
Get Kamran on GovCon podcasts (GovCon Giants, DoD Contract Academy, Govology, GovCon
Chamber). Sponsor a niche GovCon newsletter once you have a converting landing page.

### 9. Community presence
Be genuinely helpful in r/govcon, LinkedIn groups, and GovCon Slack/Discord spaces.
Answer questions, link to your free tools/content only when relevant. No spam.

## Tier 3 — Later / when budget allows

### 10. Paid ads (small, targeted)
Once a landing page converts organically, test **LinkedIn ads** (best targeting for
this ICP: job title + company size + GovCon keywords) and **Google Search ads** on
high-intent terms ("GovCon proposal software," "SAM.gov tracking tool"). Start
<$500/mo; kill losers fast.

### 11. Marketplace & directory listings
List on G2, Capterra, Product Hunt (launch day), and GovCon tool roundups. Ask beta
firms for reviews — social proof for buyers who comparison-shop.

### 12. Channel / reseller via the white-label angle
The white-label portal makes Bytescon resellable. Larger consulting firms or
GovCon service providers could resell it to their networks. Explore after
product-market fit.

### 13. Events & matchmaking
Attend (then sponsor/speak at) regional small-business matchmaking events, APEX
conferences, and GovCon summits. High cost; do once a repeatable pitch + collateral
exists.
