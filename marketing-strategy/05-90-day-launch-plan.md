# 05 · 90-Day Launch Plan

Aligned to the real timeline: **beta Nov 30, 2026 → public launch mid-December 2026**.
Today is **2026-11-10**. This plan runs ~Nov 10 → mid-Feb 2027.

> Reality check: before the public launch brings enterprise-leaning prospects, close
> the honest-marketing blockers — production file storage (R1), field encryption
> (R2), and the SOC 2 / CMMC L2 "in progress" story. Don't market security you don't
> have.

---

## Phase 0 — Pre-beta foundations (Nov 10–29) · "Load the gun"

**Goal:** everything ready so the beta instantly produces proof and pipeline.

- [ ] Finalize messaging from [01](01-positioning-and-messaging.md); write the
      landing-page copy around "built for firms with many clients, not one."
- [ ] Confirm all marketing links point to `bytesgov.com` (not dead `bytescon.com`).
- [ ] Set up the Bytescon LinkedIn company page + optimize the founder/owner's profile.
- [ ] Record a crisp **3-minute demo video** of the Discover→Qualify loop + a 60-sec
      "win-odds in one click" clip.
- [ ] Build the **200-firm ICP list** (methods in [02](02-ideal-customer-profile.md));
      start warming (follow/engage), no pitching yet.
- [ ] Ship **one free lead magnet** (Bid/No-Bid Scorecard or Quick-Score tool) with
      email capture.
- [ ] Draft 3 beta case-study templates and a testimonial-request script.
- [ ] Stand up a simple analytics + email tool (see [06](06-metrics-budget-and-tools.md)).
- [ ] Begin founder LinkedIn cadence (3–5 posts/week) — build-in-public the launch.

**Exit criteria:** landing page live, demo video done, 200-firm list warming, 1 lead
magnet live, LinkedIn running.

---

## Phase 1 — Beta (Nov 30–Dec 14) · "Make 5 firms succeed loudly"

**Goal:** 3–5 beta firms get a real win/insight; convert that into proof.

- [ ] Onboard beta firms personally (white-glove); pre-load a demo workspace each.
- [ ] Weekly 1:1 check-ins; log every friction point and fix fast.
- [ ] Capture metrics + quotes: bids scored, no-wins skipped, time saved, wins.
- [ ] Produce **1–2 case studies** + testimonial videos from early results.
- [ ] Reach out to **3 APEX Accelerators**; book a January webinar.
- [ ] Keep posting beta learnings on LinkedIn (with permission/anonymized).
- [ ] Open warmed outreach to the 200-firm list: invite to the waitlist/early access.

**Exit criteria:** ≥2 case studies, ≥3 testimonials, a waitlist started, 1 webinar
booked, public-launch blockers (R1/R2/R3) confirmed closed.

---

## Phase 2 — Public launch (mid-Dec, ~Dec 15–31) · "Make noise"

**Goal:** a visible launch spike → first self-serve trials.

- [ ] **Launch day:** Product Hunt, LinkedIn announcement, email the waitlist, post
      in GovCon communities, publish the launch blog post.
- [ ] Publish case studies prominently on the site and in the trial flow.
- [ ] Turn on the **referral incentive** (free month / bonus tokens per referral).
- [ ] Guest on 1–2 GovCon podcasts timed to launch.
- [ ] Publish the first 2 pillar SEO articles (Qualify pillar first).
- [ ] Open personalized outreach at full cadence (50 touches/week) to the ICP list.
- [ ] Monitor trials closely; fix onboarding drop-off daily.

**Exit criteria:** launch spike captured, first paying customers, trial funnel
instrumented end-to-end.

---

## Phase 3 — Early traction (Jan–mid-Feb) · "Find the repeatable loop"

**Goal:** identify the 1–2 channels that convert, then double down.

- [ ] Run the **first webinar** with an APEX partner; gate the replay.
- [ ] Publish 1–2 SEO articles/week across all five pillars + 1 "vs." comparison.
- [ ] Ship a **second free tool** (NAICS/set-aside checker).
- [ ] Launch email nurture sequences for magnet + trial leads.
- [ ] Review funnel metrics ([06](06-metrics-budget-and-tools.md)); cut what's not
      working, scale what is.
- [ ] If a landing page converts, test a **small LinkedIn ad** (<$500) to the ICP.
- [ ] Ask every happy customer for a review (G2/Capterra) + a referral.

**Exit criteria:** ≥1 channel with repeatable CAC, a growing content library, early
customer reviews, a referral loop producing leads.

## Weekly founder rhythm (sustainable for one person)

| Cadence | Do |
|---------|-----|
| **Daily** | 15 LinkedIn comments; respond to inbound/trials; 10 warm outreach touches |
| **Weekly** | 3–5 LinkedIn posts; 1–2 blog articles; beta/customer check-ins; review metrics |
| **Monthly** | 1 webinar; 1 lead magnet or free tool; 1 podcast guest spot; strategy review |
