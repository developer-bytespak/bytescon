# Bytescon, Explained Like You're 5

## The story

Imagine the **government is a giant shop owner** who needs lots of things every day:
roads fixed, computers set up, buildings cleaned, food for soldiers, websites built.

The shop owner doesn't do all that alone. Instead, they put up **notes on a big notice board** that say:
*"I need someone to fix 50 roads. Who wants to do it? Tell me your price!"*

Lots of companies — big ones and little ones — read the notes and send back a letter saying
*"Pick me! Here's why I'm good and here's my price."* That letter is called a **proposal**.
The shop owner reads all the letters and picks one winner. The winner gets paid to do the job.
That job is called a **contract**.

## The problem

For a **small company**, this is really hard:

1. 🔍 **The notice board is HUGE.** Thousands of new notes every week. Which ones fit *you*?
2. 🎲 **You don't know your chances.** Should you even try? Writing a letter takes weeks.
3. 📜 **The rules are long and tricky.** Miss one rule and your letter gets thrown away.
4. ✍️ **Writing the letter is a lot of work.** Sometimes hundreds of pages.
5. 💰 **After you win, you must keep careful records** — time, money, deadlines — or you get in trouble.

## Who helps the small companies?

There are grown-up helpers called **consulting firms**. Each consulting firm helps *many* small companies
at once. They're like coaches for a whole team.

## What Bytescon does

**Bytescon is a super-smart helper website for those coaches.** It's like giving the coach:

| Helper | What it's like | What it really does |
|--------|----------------|---------------------|
| 🔭 **Telescope** | Looks at the whole notice board every day | Pulls new government jobs from official websites and shows only the ones that fit each company |
| 🎯 **Fortune teller (an honest one)** | "You have about a 7 out of 10 chance" | Calculates the chance of winning, and shows *why* |
| ✅ **Checklist robot** | "You forgot to sign page 4!" | Finds every rule in the job notice and tracks if you followed it |
| ✏️ **Writing buddy** | Helps you write the letter | AI writes a first draft; a human must check and approve it |
| 🧮 **Calculator** | "Here's a fair price" | Builds the price and compares it with what others charged before |
| 🤝 **Friend finder** | "This company could team up with you" | Suggests partner companies to bid together |
| 📅 **Diary and piggy bank** | Remembers deadlines and money | Tracks the work, timesheets, bills, and payments after winning |
| 🤖 **9 little robot helpers** | Each watches one part | AI "agents" check things on a timer and raise their hand when a human should decide |

## The most important rule

**The robots suggest. People decide.**
Bytescon's AI helpers never send a proposal, sign a paper, approve a bill, or spend money by themselves.
They always hand it to a person to say *yes* or *no*.

## Who can log in?

- **The coach team** (consulting firm staff) — they use the main website.
- **The small companies** (clients) — they get their own little window (the **Client Portal**) to upload papers and see progress.
- **Partner companies** (subcontractors) — they get their own window too (the **Partner Portal**) to send their parts of the work.
- **The Bytescon owners** — they have a special admin view to watch the whole system.

## One sentence to remember

> **Bytescon helps small businesses find government jobs, know their chances, follow the rules,
> write a great proposal, and do the job right — with robot helpers that always ask a human first.**
